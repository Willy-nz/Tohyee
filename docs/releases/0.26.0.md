## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migrations 0222, 0223 and 0225).
- **Shopify, after updating:**
  1. Settings › Sales platforms › your store › **Sync now**. Payouts that were skipped are read again and post (fees to the fees account, the net to the bank). Variants without a SKU come in as `SHOPIFY-…` items, and orders that were waiting on a single-name customer post.
  2. On the store card, choose **Only customers who have ordered** if you don't want every Shopify sign-up as a contact. Contacts already brought in stay.
  3. **Change posting settings** › Payment methods: create Afterpay and PayPal clearing accounts; leave manual payments as "left owing". A gateway only appears there after an order using it comes in, so new Afterpay or PayPal orders wait until it's set.
  4. Orders already posted aren't rewritten: Afterpay and PayPal orders from before stay in Shopify clearing. Move them with one transfer per provider if you want.
- **AI apps:** after a connection's access changes, the AI app is told its tools are out of date. If its tool list still doesn't change, disconnect and reconnect Tohyee in the AI app.
- **Not yet tried for real:** the payout, gateway and import fixes were tested only with made-up Shopify-shaped data.

## New

- **Reports like Xero:** each report has a proper heading (title, organisation, period), a clean statement style with lines above totals and a double line under net profit, and one toolbar with date presets, More and Export. Zero cells are blank, amounts stay readable on a phone, and PDFs show NZ time and indenting (#562).
- **Compare and filter:** compare the Profit and loss and Balance sheet with earlier months, quarters or years, add Total, Year to date and % of trading income columns, and filter every financial report by tracking. Custom and published reports drill down to account transactions (#563).
- **Edit layout:** the Profit and loss and Balance sheet have an **Edit layout** button that starts from the standard layout. Drag rows, rename in place, group accounts, hide rows, add notes and page breaks, and add formulas (×, ÷, % of). Save named copies and set one as the default. Saved reports appear in the report picker with favourites (#564).
- **Shopify payouts post:** the payout's own transfer row is no longer treated as unknown, withdrawals are handled, and a missing account makes a payout wait instead of being skipped for good. Payouts skipped before are read again, and the Payouts line has **Try again** (#560).
- **Payment methods for Shopify:** Afterpay, PayPal and manual payments each go to their own account instead of Shopify clearing (#560).
- **Shopify imports:** products without a SKU come in by Shopify's own ID; "Only customers who have ordered"; test customers skipped; names taken from the order when Shopify has none; single-name clashes get their own contact instead of blocking orders; orders post oldest first so invoice numbers run in date order (#561).
- **AI and fixed assets:** the AI can create fixed asset types and set an asset's tax details, including Investment Boost, with the same checks as the Income tax page (#559).
- **AI reports:** compare and tracking options on the AI's report tools, plus budget vs actual and custom report tools (#563, #564).

## Changed

- **Account transactions** for income and expense accounts open at the start of the financial year, matching the trial balance (#563).
- **AI tool lists refresh:** the AI is told when its tools change, and get_organisation says when its list is out of date (#558).

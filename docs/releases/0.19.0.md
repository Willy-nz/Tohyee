## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migration 0207).
- **Farm has moved:** it's now its own app beside Accounting, CRM and Analytics (the app switcher, the phone ☰ menu and Ctrl K). Accounting's top bar no longer has a Farm menu. Every page keeps its address and permissions.
- **Not yet reviewed by Jess:** the choices in decisions 1110–1121, 1124 and 1125–1134. Sharing locations may need a line in your own staff privacy notice.
- **Not yet tried for real:** Firefox, Safari and real phones (checked in Chrome only), and location sharing between real phones.

## New

- **Farm as its own app:** its top bar is Today · Map · Log · Safety · Effluent · More. On a phone, the bottom tabs now stay on every farm page, including direct links, reloads and pages opened offline. More opens with a Farm menu of big buttons, with Effluent first.
- **"Use my location" on every map:** farm map, Today, hazards, effluent and tractor. It shows whether location is allowed, blocked, unavailable or timed out, with that browser's steps to allow it again and a Check again button. It shows how accurate the fix is. Centring the map on yourself never shares your location.
- **Farm team on the map (opt-in):** "Share my location…" shows a short privacy notice, then shares for 1 to 12 hours (4 by default), with a Stop sharing banner on every farm page. It only sends while Tohyee is open on screen. Only the latest position is kept, it's deleted when sharing ends (never kept past 24 hours), it's left out of exports, AI, Analytics, backups and the offline copy, and only people with access to that farm can see it. Markers go grey as "last known" after 5 minutes.
- **Install steps for your browser:** the "Get Tohyee on this device" page picks the right steps for iPhone Safari, Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet and Firefox on Android (⋮ › Install), and says honestly where a browser can't install. It's a home-screen web app, not an APK.

## Changed

- **Safety:** the large Call 111 button is gone. An "Emergency details" card has the farm's address, entrance, contacts to tap, and 111.
- **Tractor mode:** a "Limited — keep Tohyee open and your screen unlocked while recording" note shows before Start and beside the recording controls. No other tractor changes.

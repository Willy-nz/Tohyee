## Before you update

- **Security: update soon.** Next.js is updated to 16.4.1, fixing two critical problems that could let someone run their own code on a server, and one that could crash it (#435).
- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (core migrations 0021–0023 and 0025, tenant migrations 0186–0191).
- **Old-style backups can't be opened any more.** Only backups made with the recovery key (since 0.12.0) restore; older files are left alone (decisions 867–869).
- **The demo farm has moved** from the organisation menu in the books to the server's Organisations page (decisions 880–884).
- **Farm pages look different on a phone:** Today · Map · Log · Safety · More along the bottom.
- **Not yet reviewed by Jess:** the farm workspace choices (decisions 900–913), the Farm staff role, and the farm safety wording. `docs/FARM-SAFETY.md` lists the questions, including what WorkSafe needs. Farm safety keeps records; it doesn't decide what the law needs you to keep.
- **Not yet tried for real:** passkeys on a real phone, the weekly safety email with a real mail account, and the farm walk offline on a real phone.

## New

- **Farm safety:** in Farming › Safety:
  - hazards and no-go zones on the farm map, with review dates;
  - near misses and incidents, with actions and photos, and a prompt when WorkSafe may need to be told (never sent automatically);
  - inductions with sign-offs on the person's own phone, who's on site now, and a gate sign-in link for visitors;
  - pre-start checks on machinery, a training register with expiry reminders, and chemicals with their safety data sheets;
  - emergency details.
  
  Records can't be quietly changed: every edit keeps the earlier version, nothing is deleted, and a history check shows if records were changed in the database. A **WorkSafe pack** exports a date range with its full history, and a **weekly copy** is emailed to addresses outside the farm. Injury and medical details are restricted (decisions 930–959, `docs/FARM-SAFETY.md`).
- **Farmer-friendly farm screens:** on a phone, **Today** (jobs, mobs in, safety due, a small map), **Map** (paddocks coloured by days since grazed or by how old the cover reading is, with a paddock card), **Log** (one question at a time: mob in, mob out, cover reading, feed, and safety reports), **Safety** and **More** (the detailed tables). A **farm walk** saves each reading on the phone as you go, then sends it; sending twice never makes two (decisions 900–913).
- **Farm staff role:** staff record field work and safety reports on the farms chosen for them, without seeing the books, reports, money or other farms.
- **Sign in with a passkey:** Face ID, fingerprint or Windows Hello, added from Your account after a normal sign-in. Only on Tohyee's web address (Remote access). Changing your password removes your passkeys (decisions 885–899).
- **Invite people from People and roles:** new people get a setup link by email, and show as Invited until they sign in (decisions 870–879).

## Changed

- **Farm pages:** histories load 25 at a time with filters, so long pages like Pasture stay quick; each page opens on the farm you last used; one line per record on a phone; plainer words.
- **Demo farm:** added, reset and deleted from the Windows server app's Organisations page or the browser's server settings, by server admins.
- **Backups:** only the recovery key and recovery kit.
- **Email:** nodemailer updated to 10.2.1 (#444).

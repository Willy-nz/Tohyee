Everything merged since 0.13.0 (#433, #434 and #438). It includes the security fixes prepared as 0.13.1, which wasn't published separately.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (core migration 0020).
- **Security fixes:** update soon if you use Sign in with Google, AI app connections or the PowerPoint add-in (see Fixed).
- **Restoring people from a backup:** the backup's role in each organisation now wins over the replaced account's, the preview lists each change, and a restore that would leave an organisation without an Owner is refused (decision 854).
- **The demo farm is on by default.** To hide it, set `TOHYEE_DEMO_FARM=off` in the server's settings file. It will be taken out in a later release.

## New

- **Try the demo farm:** anyone signed in can add Kōwhai Dairies Ltd, a made-up Otago dairy farm, from the organisation menu ("Try the demo farm…"), or a server admin with `tohyee-admin demo add`. It takes about half a minute and is marked DEMO on every page. It has 12 to 23 months of data up to today:
  - books with reconciled bank statements, co-op milk income, bills, two-monthly GST returns, fixed assets and a season budget;
  - fortnightly payroll for two staff (when the server has IRD rates for the dates and `TOHYEE_SECRET_KEY`);
  - two farms with paddock maps, farm walks, grazings, supplements, fertiliser, soil tests, livestock movements, co-op shares, the farm calendar and notebook;
  - Analytics reports: Farm performance (milk solids, pasture, working expenses per kg MS), Accounts (profit, cash and GST), Farm, and Business overview.
  
  Everything is made up and entered through Tohyee's own screens behind the scenes, so the books balance and reports work as with real data. It never sends email or connects to banks, IRD, mailboxes or anything else, and can't have other members. Reset puts it back to fresh; Delete removes it. One per person (decisions 855–866, `docs/DEMO-FARM.md`).

## Fixed

- **Removing someone's Google sign-in now disconnects their apps.** When a server admin removes Google sign-in, every AI app, AI key, farm app key and PowerPoint add-in sign-in for that person ends at the same time, and the person's email says so. Their password still works. If you unlink your own Google account, your apps stay connected and the email says how to disconnect them (decisions 850, 852).
- **Ending access can't be raced.** An app finishing its connection at the moment a password is changed or reset, two-step is reset, Google sign-in is removed or someone is removed from an organisation no longer ends up connected (decision 853).
- **Restoring people keeps their Google sign-in.** It's moved across properly, a clash with another login is reported on the restore page, and nobody is left marked Google-only without a Google link (decision 851).

## Known gaps

- The demo farm's GST payments are entered as journals, and its reports have no "last year" comparisons (its books start on 1 June last year).
- `tohyee-admin demo add` hasn't been tried from the Windows or Docker install yet; the organisation menu works everywhere.
- Nightly backups include demo farms.
- Private mail that a replaced account connected on the replacement computer isn't moved to the restored person yet.

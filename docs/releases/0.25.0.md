Everything merged since 0.23.0 (#529, #533–#536, #540, #541 and #549–#556). It includes what was prepared as 0.24.0, which wasn't published separately.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migrations 0214 to 0221).
- **Confirm your GST registration.** Tohyee no longer assumes every organisation is GST registered. Settings asks Yes / No with nothing ticked until someone confirms (an organisation already registered with a GST number in Settings counts as confirmed). Until it's confirmed, automatic posting from Shopify, WooCommerce and the AI doesn't charge or claim GST.
- **Shopify's new data needs new permissions.** Settings › Sales platforms shows every scope in one line with a Copy button: paste it into your app's access scopes in Shopify's Dev Dashboard, release and install the new app version, then press **Test connection** and **Sync now**. Shop stats also need Shopify to approve "protected customer data, level 2".
- **Not yet tried for real:** the guided shop setup, Shopify extras and payout matching, the Akahu setup, and mixed-use accounts have only been tried against made-up data, not a real store, bank feed or card.
- **Fewer words on screen:** page subtitle sentences and repeated titles are gone across the app. Warnings, things you can't undo and privacy notes are still shown, as one line.

## New

- **GST registration you confirm:** Yes / No / Not sure when creating an organisation; registration dates are used per transaction. Changing it later never rewrites what's posted: Tohyee lists the months affected for you to correct (#549).
- **Guided Akahu bank feeds:** Connect → choose accounts (add one on the spot) → start date → check → first sync. The card shows "Accounts need linking" with a **Link bank accounts** button, and tells "synced, nothing new" apart from "never synced" (#550).
- **Unreconcile selected:** tick reconciled lines on Statement lines and unreconcile them together after a preview. Nothing is posted or voided. A Full access AI can do the same through the same checks (#551).
- **Bank batches:** AI reconciliation runs are saved as batches you can see, resume where they stopped without posting twice, and undo after a preview (#551).
- **AI bank coding to review:** the AI can propose how to code bank lines without posting anything. A person edits, rejects or approves them on the new Proposals tab, with totals by account and tax code; approval posts each line exactly once (#556).
- **Shopify payouts matched in the bank:** a Shopify payout deposit is matched against "Money held by Shopify" with its sales, refunds and fees shown, never posting sales twice. Unknown payouts stay unmatched with the reason (#554).
- **Personal and mixed-use cards:** mark a bank or card account as business, mixed or personal. On mixed accounts each line is reviewed as business, private, split or refund. The business part is expensed and paid by the owner; private lines post nothing; the personal debt never becomes a business liability (#555).
- **Guided shop setup:** Settings › Sales platforms starts with "Which shop do you use?" (Shopify or WooCommerce) and goes one step at a time: connect, choose what to bring in, start date, accounts, check, turn on. Suggested accounts are filled in, and you can add a new account code right there without losing your place. "Money held by Shopify" (Shopify clearing) is explained and can be created for you. A worked $100 sale with a $3 fee shows where each amount goes. Nothing is saved until the end, and posting only turns on if you choose it (#535).
- **What each part brought in:** each store shows Customers, Products, Orders and Payouts as off, needs setting up, up to date, partly brought in or failed, with real counts and reasons (for example "12 skipped: no SKU"). The sync log loads older entries and can be filtered (#535).
- **Customers to check:** a Shopify or WooCommerce customer with the same name as one of your contacts but a different email is no longer skipped with advice to change the contact's email. Choose **Same person** to link them (the contact's details aren't changed) or **Different person** to add a new contact (#541).
- **More Shopify data (read only):** stock levels per location with a **Compare stock** view against Tohyee, daily shop stats, discounts, gift cards (only the last 4 characters are kept) and fulfilments with tracking. A new **Shopify** source in Analytics has tables for all of it plus orders, refunds and payouts. Nothing new is posted to the accounts and Tohyee's stock is never changed from Shopify (#533).
- **Pictures for every menu item:** a properly drawn cog, and every page in the Settings, Tax, Sales, Purchases, Banking, Payroll, Reports and other menus now has its own picture instead of one repeated icon (#529, #536).
- **AI can check your shop setup:** two read-only AI tools show a store's setup and sync log, so the AI can tell you why something didn't come through. They never show keys or secrets and can't change anything (#534).

## Changed

- **Simpler screens:** no sentence under page headings, lists without a box repeating the title, fewer card descriptions and hints. The invoice has one **Record payment** button with a "⋯" menu and totals on the right; Home, Reports (one report picker), Settings (one hub, Modules on its own page), Farm pages and the Analytics editor are simpler too (#552, #553).
- **AI connections report their real access level** instead of always saying "read-only" (#534).
- **AI edits keep everything on a line:** editing a draft invoice, bill, quote or order line no longer drops its item, unit, tracking or custom fields. An edit that would lose something is refused with nothing changed (#540).
- **AI lists page properly:** the AI can page through every invoice, bill, quote, order and other list, and never thinks a partial list is the whole thing (#540).
- **Settings › Email:** "How documents are sent" is now a choice of tiles, and server details are hidden when Gmail or Microsoft 365 fills them in (#535).

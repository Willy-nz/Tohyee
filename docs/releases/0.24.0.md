## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migrations 0214 and 0215).
- **Shopify's new data needs new permissions.** Settings › Sales platforms shows every scope in one line with a Copy button: paste it into your app's access scopes in Shopify's Dev Dashboard, release and install the new app version, then press **Test connection** and **Sync now**. Shop stats also need Shopify to approve "protected customer data, level 2" for your app; until then the sync log says so and everything else still works.
- **Not yet tried for real:** the guided setup, stock comparison, shop stats, discounts, gift cards and fulfilments have only been tried against Shopify-shaped test data, not a real store.

## New

- **Guided shop setup:** Settings › Sales platforms starts with "Which shop do you use?" (Shopify or WooCommerce) and goes one step at a time: connect, choose what to bring in, start date, accounts, check, turn on. Suggested accounts are filled in, and you can add a new account code right there without losing your place. "Money held by Shopify" (Shopify clearing) is explained and can be created for you. A worked $100 sale with a $3 fee shows where each amount goes. Nothing is saved until the end, and posting only turns on if you choose it (#535).
- **What each part brought in:** each store shows Customers, Products, Orders and Payouts as off, needs setting up, up to date, partly brought in or failed, with real counts and reasons (for example "12 skipped: no SKU"). The sync log loads older entries and can be filtered (#535).
- **Customers to check:** a Shopify or WooCommerce customer with the same name as one of your contacts but a different email is no longer skipped with advice to change the contact's email. Choose **Same person** to link them (the contact's details aren't changed) or **Different person** to add a new contact (#541).
- **More Shopify data (read only):** stock levels per location with a **Compare stock** view against Tohyee, daily shop stats, discounts, gift cards (only the last 4 characters are kept) and fulfilments with tracking. A new **Shopify** source in Analytics has tables for all of it plus orders, refunds and payouts. Nothing new is posted to the accounts and Tohyee's stock is never changed from Shopify (#533).
- **Pictures for every menu item:** a properly drawn cog, and every page in the Settings, Tax, Sales, Purchases, Banking, Payroll, Reports and other menus now has its own picture instead of one repeated icon (#529, #536).
- **AI can check your shop setup:** two read-only AI tools show a store's setup and sync log, so the AI can tell you why something didn't come through. They never show keys or secrets and can't change anything (#534).

## Changed

- **AI connections report their real access level** instead of always saying "read-only" (#534).
- **AI edits keep everything on a line:** editing a draft invoice, bill, quote or order line no longer drops its item, unit, tracking or custom fields. An edit that would lose something is refused with nothing changed (#540).
- **AI lists page properly:** the AI can page through every invoice, bill, quote, order and other list, and never thinks a partial list is the whole thing (#540).
- **Settings › Email:** "How documents are sent" is now a choice of tiles, and server details are hidden when Gmail or Microsoft 365 fills them in (#535).

## Before you update

- **Logins without two-step sign-in:** a password alone is no longer enough once two-step is on. For each login that never set up two-step, use "Send setup link" on the server app's Users page. The person chooses their own password and sets up two-step from the link, which lasts 7 days ([#212](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/212)).
- **Remote access without a secret key:** if your server has remote access on (Cloudflare or Tailscale Funnel) but no `TOHYEE_SECRET_KEY`, sign-ins through remote access are now refused, because two-step sign-in can't work without the key. On your own network, sign-ins work as before. The server app's Home warns about this ([#212](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/212)).
- **Windows:**
  - The Tohyee service now runs as its own limited account (`NT SERVICE\Tohyee`) instead of SYSTEM, and the database uses two ordinary logins instead of the superuser ([#185](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/185), [#213](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/213)).
  - The installer gives the service the backup, analytics and bank-file folders you've already chosen.
- **GST registration:** existing organisations stay registered for GST. The only exception is an organisation with no GST number and nothing ever posted to GST, which is switched to "not registered" ([#194](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/194)).
- **R&D income years for early balance dates:** for organisations with a balance date before 31 March, saved R&D years (activities, approvals, tax depreciation) move forward one year to follow IRD's numbering. For example, a year ending 30 November 2025 now counts toward the 2025-26 income year (ending 31 March 2026) ([#247](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/247)).
- Database upgrades run by themselves when Tohyee starts. Back up first if you install by hand.

## New

**Farming ([#249](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/249)), an optional module inside Accounting** (Settings › Modules)

- **Farms and paddocks:** each paddock keeps the effective area you farm beside its mapped area. Boundaries are versioned, and splitting or merging paddocks keeps their history ([#252](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/252)).
- **Paddock shapes:** import from GeoJSON, KML or KMZ (Google Earth) or a zipped shapefile, or draw them on the map. Shapes in NZTM2000 are converted ([#252](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/252), [#256](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/256)).
- **Aerial imagery** from LINZ Basemaps under the paddock map, using your organisation's own free LINZ key ([#254](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/254)).
- **Fertiliser plans** (examples FB1–FB8):
  - Versioned plans by paddock.
  - Bills linked as product bought and spreading.
  - Applications entered or imported from a CSV.
  - Planned against actual by product and paddock: kg N/ha, cost and cost a hectare.
  - Fertiliser is expensed when bought; nothing in the plan posts ([#253](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/253)).
- **Soil tests:** import a lab's CSV or Excel file with a preview, or enter one by hand, and attach the lab's report. Results are kept exactly as the lab reported them ([#255](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/255)).
- **Analytics:** paddocks, fertiliser and soil results are copied as `tohyee_farm_*` tables ([#254](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/254)).

**Livestock for tax ([#221](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/221)), an optional module**

- Herd scheme, national standard cost and market value for sheep, beef, dairy, deer and goats.
- Movements and head counts, the year-end valuation and its journal, and printed workings.
- Agent account sales, high-priced livestock, management values and elections (examples LV1–LV26; [#232](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/232)–[#242](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/242)).

**Jobs, rosters and clocking in**

- **Jobs and visits** inside Projects: schedule a visit, tick its checklist, and turn it into time (examples JV1–JV9; [#244](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/244)).
- **Rosters** inside Payroll:
  - Areas with weekly budgets.
  - Shifts costed including KiwiSaver.
  - Staff unavailability.
  - Published rosters that staff see under My shifts (RS1–RS4; [#245](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/245)).
  - Publishing emails each person their shifts ([#261](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/261)).
- **Clocking in** on a shared device with PINs and breaks; clock-ins become timesheets (RS5–RS8; [#246](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/246)).

**CRM ([#216](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/216), stages 1–3)**

- **Sales roles:**
  - **Sales rep** and **Sales manager** roles are CRM-only, with no access to the books or payroll.
  - Admins make sales teams.
  - Reps see every company and person, but only their own deals, leads and tasks. Managers also see their team's.
- **Leads:**
  - **Getting leads in:** a separate Lead record, typed in or imported from CSV/Excel. Leads also come from a web form on your website (with a hidden spam trap and rate limits) and from a mailbox folder or Gmail label.
  - **Working them:** a lead is qualified or marked unqualified, then converted into a company, a person and a deal, keeping its history.
- **Duplicates:**
  - Tohyee suggests likely duplicate companies and people.
  - A company with nothing in the books can be merged into another. Two that both have accounting records are linked as the same customer instead.
  - Adding a company or a lead warns you if something similar already exists.
- **Follow-up rules:** automatic tasks when a lead arrives, a deal reaches a stage, a deal goes quiet, or a task is overdue. Each event only ever makes one task.
- **Sales emails:**
  - Send from your own Gmail or Microsoft 365 mailbox, using templates with merge fields. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
  - "Don't email" on leads and people.
  - **Sequences:** steps that become tasks on their days, and stop when the person replies.
- **Campaigns:**
  - Each campaign has a budget and an actual cost (for reports only; nothing goes into the books).
  - Each lead and deal has one source campaign. Members go from added to sent to responded.
  - Each campaign has a report, including cost per lead and cost per won deal.
- **Team forecasts:**
  - Each team's figures are added up.
  - Managers can adjust a member's figures; every adjustment needs a reason and is kept.
  - Forecasts can be submitted and kept to compare later.
- **Sales dashboard:**
  - Lead conversion, win rate, sales cycle, open deals by stage, activity, quota attainment and campaign results.
  - Sales roles see only their own figures.
- **Deal products:**
  - Product lines on a deal set its amount.
  - "Make quote" turns them into a draft quote, and "New revision" replaces the open quote.
  - Accepting a quote wins the deal and links the invoice or sales order.

**Accounting**

- **Line discounts:** a "Disc %" on invoice, quote, sales order, repeating invoice and credit note lines. GST is worked out on the discounted amount. The discount shows on screen, printed documents and PDFs, and is kept when one document is made from another (examples DS1–DS6).
- **Not registered for GST:** a switch with registration dates. While an organisation isn't registered, GST codes are refused and GST reports are hidden ([#180](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/180)).
- **Exchange rates:** choose where rates come from (ECB, uploaded rate sets such as IRD monthly averages, or typed only). Every rate shows its source ([#183](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/183)).
- **Accounting as a module:** pick Accounting, Tax, CRM and Analytics when creating an organisation. A CRM-only or Analytics-only organisation is possible ([#181](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/181)).
- **Bank feeds:** several named logins per provider (Akahu, SimpleFIN, Stripe, PayPal, Wise), for example two people's BNZ logins ([#182](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/issues/182)).
- **Direct debit with GoCardless (BECS NZ):** customers sign an authority through GoCardless's page. Invoices are collected on their due date, and payouts and fees are posted ([#206](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/206)).
- **Feed health:** a bank feed that hasn't synced for 48 hours is marked "not syncing", and the top bar counts failed and stale feeds from every provider ([#263](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/263)).
- **Transfers between your own accounts:** when reconciling, the matching line on your other account (opposite amount, within 2 days) is suggested, and one transfer reconciles both lines ([#264](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/264)).
- **Joint accounts:** an Akahu account is also recognised by its bank account number, so a joint account can't be linked twice through two people's logins ([#266](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/266)).
- **Year-end accountant pack:** one ZIP with the trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet and general ledger as CSV, plus a bank reconciliation PDF for each bank and card account ([#265](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/265)).
- **Balance sheet:** current and non-current subtotals and net assets ([#267](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/267)).
- **Statement of changes in equity and statement of cash flows** (indirect method), on screen and in the year-end pack.
- **GST part payments explained:** on the payments and hybrid bases, each part-paid line's GST opens to show paid ÷ total × line GST.
- **Recode a posted line:** change an invoice, bill or bank transaction line's account or tracking with a reason, instead of voiding the document. A correcting journal is posted on the document's date.
- **Landed cost on a bill's stock:** freight, duty or insurance spread by value or quantity; the share for stock already sold goes to cost of sales.
- **Livestock killed for rations:** its own head count column. The owner's rations post at market value (Dr drawings, Cr rations income); staff rations post nothing.
- **Keyboard entry:** dates can be typed NZ-style with shorthand (1410, t, +14, +2w), and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter saves a form ([#262](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/262)).

## Security

- **Next.js 16.3.8**, with its security fixes: one high (server-side request forgery in image optimisation) and several medium ([#220](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/220)).
- **Remote access:**
  - The sign-in limit can't be dodged with a forged address.
  - The emailed two-step reset counts towards lockout.
  - The server settings address refuses anything coming through a tunnel.
  - HSTS is on.
- **Setup links instead of passwords:** with two-step on, new logins get a one-time setup link instead of a password. An organisation can be handed over to a new owner ([#212](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/212)).
- **Sign-in monitor:**
  - Every sign-in is recorded.
  - Unusual ones (a new browser, repeated failures, a locked login, a backup code, an admin signing in through remote access) are emailed and shown on a new Sign-ins page.
  - You can see and end signed-in sessions ([#214](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/214)).
- **Health check:** through remote access, `/api/health` no longer shows the version.
- **Cloudflare credentials:** leftover Cloudflare sign-in files are deleted.
- **Remote access pages:** they now say plainly who can see the traffic ([#219](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/219)).

## Fixes

- **Lists sorted as text:** once ids passed 9, several lists and "latest" lookups sorted ids as text, so "9" came after "10". This affected:
  - Home's recent activity
  - payroll's latest leave liability posting
  - statement line order
  - which bank feed login counted as first ([#215](https://github.com/Willy-nz/Tohyee/pull/215))

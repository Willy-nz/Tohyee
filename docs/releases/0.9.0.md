Changes on main since 0.8.0 (not released yet).

## New

- **Price changes on repeating invoices** (Sales › Repeating invoices › Change prices):
  - Raise or lower prices on many repeating invoices at once, by a percentage or to a fixed price for one item.
  - Optional minimum increase and rounding to 50c, $1, $5 or $10.
  - New prices from a date you choose; earlier invoices still to be made keep the old price.
  - A preview of every line and invoice total before saving, and an undo.
  - Examples RP1–RP8, not yet approved (decision 535).
- **Tier 3 and Tier 4 not-for-profit performance reports** (Reports › Financial statements):
  - Choose the format: special purpose statements, Tier 3 (accrual) or Tier 4 (cash).
  - Entity information and a statement of service performance with this year's and last year's measures.
  - Statements in XRB's minimum categories, with last year beside this year. Each account's category is suggested until you choose one.
  - Cash shown by what it was for, including customer and supplier payments split by the invoices and bills they paid.
  - Draft notes suited to each tier.
  - Examples NPR1–NPR10, not yet reviewed (decision 536).

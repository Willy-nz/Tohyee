Changes on main since 0.8.0 (not released yet).

## New

- **Price changes on repeating invoices** (Sales › Repeating invoices › Change prices):
  - Raise or lower prices on many repeating invoices at once, by a percentage or to a fixed price for one item.
  - Optional minimum increase and rounding to 50c, $1, $5 or $10.
  - New prices from a date you choose; earlier invoices still to be made keep the old price.
  - A preview of every line and invoice total before saving, and an undo.
  - Examples RP1–RP8, not yet approved (decision 535).

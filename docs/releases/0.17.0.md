- **Old-style backups can't be opened any more.** Only backups made with the recovery key (since 0.12.0) restore; older files are left alone (decisions 867–869).
- **The demo farm has moved** from the organisation menu in the books to the server's Organisations page (decisions 880–884).
- **Farm pages look different on a phone:** Today · Map · Log · Safety · More along the bottom.
- **Not yet reviewed by Jess:** the farm workspace choices (decisions 900–913), the Farm staff role, and the farm safety wording. `docs/FARM-SAFETY.md` lists the questions, including what WorkSafe needs. Farm safety keeps records; it doesn't decide what the law needs you to keep.
- **Not yet tried for real:** the phone app, passkeys on a real phone, the weekly safety email with a real mail account, and the farm walk offline on a real phone.

## New: Tohyee on your phone (#472)

- **Get Tohyee on this device:** open your Tohyee web address on your phone and follow "Get Tohyee on this device". On an iPhone, use Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen; on Android, use Chrome › Install. No app store. It needs Tohyee's https web address (Remote access) or a Tailscale HTTPS address; a home-network address like 192.168.x.x won't work (`docs/MOBILE-APP.md`).
- **Farm information kept offline automatically:** after you sign in, your farms' maps, paddocks, the last 12 months of pasture, grazing and feed, plans, soil, jobs, equipment and checklists, and safety information are kept on the phone and refreshed while it's open. Each part shows when it was last updated. Injury and medical details, mail, the books and payroll are never kept on the phone.
- **Work without a signal:** logs, photos with location, safety reports, pre-start checks, induction sign-offs and sign-in are kept on the phone and sent once, when you're back in signal. Each shows Saved on this device, Waiting to send, Synced or Needs attention. Approvals, like putting a machine back in use, always need a connection.
- **Tractor and spreader mode:** choose the farm, paddock and equipment (working width), then Start, Working on/off, Pause and Finish. A full-screen map shows the worked area, overlaps (counted once) and GPS gaps. A finished job goes into the paddock diary and is sent when there's signal; you can export it as GeoJSON or GPX. It records only while the screen is on (keep the phone mounted and awake), and says clearly if recording stops. Phone GPS is an estimate, not proof of application.
- **Not yet tried on a real phone or in a paddock.** Starting a new tractor job needs a signal (recording and finishing work without one).

## Also new

- **Farm safety:** in Farming › Safety:
  - hazards and no-go zones on the farm map, with review dates;
  - near misses and incidents, with actions and photos, and a prompt when WorkSafe may need to be told (never sent automatically);
  - inductions with sign-offs on the person's own phone, who's on site now, and a gate sign-in link for visitors;
  - pre-start checks on machinery, a training register with expiry reminders, and chemicals with their safety data sheets;
  - emergency details.
  
  Safety opens on an overview built for a phone: emergency details with Call 111, three big buttons (Report something, Check equipment, Sign in / induction), what needs doing, and a machine's QR code opening its checklist (#469). Notifiable-event records show how long to keep them, gate-link sign-offs are marked self-declared, and the gate page has a full privacy notice (#467). Records can't be quietly changed: every edit keeps the earlier version, nothing is deleted, and a history check shows if records were changed in the database. A **WorkSafe pack** exports a date range with its full history, and a **weekly copy** is emailed to addresses outside the farm. Injury and medical details are restricted (decisions 930–959, `docs/FARM-SAFETY.md`).
- **Farmer-friendly farm screens:** on a phone, **Today** (jobs, mobs in, safety due, a small map), **Map** (paddocks coloured by days since grazed or by how old the cover reading is, with a paddock card), **Log** (one question at a time: mob in, mob out, cover reading, feed, and safety reports), **Safety** and **More** (the detailed tables). A **farm walk** saves each reading on the phone as you go, then sends it; sending twice never makes two (decisions 900–913).
- **Farm staff role:** staff record field work and safety reports on the farms chosen for them, without seeing the books, reports, money or other farms.
- **Sign in with a passkey:** Face ID, fingerprint or Windows Hello, added from Your account after a normal sign-in. Only on Tohyee's web address (Remote access). Changing your password removes your passkeys (decisions 885–899).
- **Invite people from People and roles:** new people get a setup link by email, and show as Invited until they sign in (decisions 870–879).

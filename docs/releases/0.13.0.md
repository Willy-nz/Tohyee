## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (core migrations 0018 and 0019).
- **Both new features need Remote access** (a proper https web address). With it off, the AI page and Settings say so, and AI keys keep working as before.
- **The AI page has a new layout.** It's much shorter: "Connect your AI" first, then connected apps and keys. The Claude Desktop setup, technical details and the list of what AI can do are folded under "More". Existing AI keys keep working unchanged.
- **Not yet tried for real:** connecting Claude.ai or ChatGPT by signing in, and the PowerPoint add-in in real PowerPoint. Tell us what you see if either doesn't work.
- **Not yet reviewed by Jess:** the sign-in details (an hour for access, 60 days for renewal, as Xero does) and the PowerPoint add-in's choices (decisions 810–823 and 830–843).

## New

- **Connect your AI by signing in:** Claude.ai, ChatGPT and other AI apps connect by pasting Tohyee's address and signing in to Tohyee (password and two-step, or Google), instead of copying a key. You choose the organisation and what the app may do, with the same limits as keys (Full access for Owners only, after a warning). Each connected app is listed on the AI page and can be disconnected straight away, and you get an email when one connects. Private mail stays off for a connection unless you turn it on (decisions 810–823).
- **PowerPoint add-in:** put a live Tohyee Analytics report, or one chart, on a slide, as Power BI's add-in does. Slicers, click-to-filter, drill-down, page tabs and bookmarks work while editing and presenting. Each viewer signs in as themselves and sees only what they can see in Tohyee; anyone who can't sign in sees the last snapshot, with "Sign in to see live data". The add-in only reads, and the presentation never holds a login. An admin downloads it from Settings › PowerPoint add-in and uploads it in PowerPoint (My Add-ins › Upload My Add-in) or shares it across Microsoft 365. Your account › Connected apps lists its sign-ins (decisions 830–843, `docs/POWERPOINT-ADDIN.md`).

Everything merged since 0.34.0 (#673–#687, apart from #676).

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations 0278 to 0281.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the new livestock worked examples (LV25B, LV29-LV32) and the farm Log's (LS1-LS5) are Claude's, built from IRD's determinations and OS 25/02, and not yet checked.
- **Livestock under NSC may value young stock differently:** rising one-year stock now follows IRD's own formula (clause 3 of the 1993 NSC determination). Only a class with both bought stock and deaths in the year comes out differently. An approved valuation isn't changed. Check a year you haven't approved yet (#682).

## New

- **Farm Log: stock and treatments:** move a mob, births, deaths and treatments, one question a screen, saved on the phone first (#674).
- **"+ New" has farm items** while Farming is on. Farm staff now see these, and only these (#674).
- **The AI can do what the screens do (#685):**
  - **Full access:** void, delete drafts, archive, dispose of assets, roll back depreciation, exclude bank lines, set the lock date. Each of these shows what it will change and needs a second, confirmed call. AI apps are told they're destructive (#687).
  - **Make and post:** move quotes and orders on (accept, decline, invoice, close, cancel).
  - **Owners and admins:** settings such as GST codes, payment terms and custom fields.
  - **Check your AI connections' access levels after updating.**
- **The AI can correct a manual journal** as Edit this journal does: a reversal and the new version, after a preview. Full access only (#673).
- **Server › Logs:** server admins can read the server's log files in Tohyee, with a search and an "errors only" filter. Only on the server computer, and read-only. The Windows service now says where its logs are (#684).
- **Pictures on empty lists:** a page's main list shows a small picture when it's empty (#675).
- **Livestock:**
  - high-priced animals bought before livestock's first year are carried in (#677);
  - bobby calves bought under NSC carry IRD's bobby calf cost (#678);
  - pigs, under the herd scheme (#680);
  - stock bought from an associated person who used the herd scheme must stay in it (#681).

## Changed

- **Rising one-year stock under NSC follows IRD's formula:** animals that die no longer take an average share of the value with them. Dairy calves sold as bobby calves can be marked so, and IRD's formula leaves them out (#682).

## Still not built

- Signed updates (#676) wait for the signing key to be added on GitHub (#683).

Everything merged since 0.9.0 (#289–#325).

## Before you update

- **Nothing to do for most servers.** The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.
- **Farm calendar:** to connect a mailbox, an admin adds this server's address followed by `/api/farming/mail/callback` to the Google or Microsoft app's sign-in (redirect) addresses, the same app used under Settings › Email.
- **Remote access** now pauses itself for an hour after 20 failed sign-ins in 10 minutes from outside; resume early on the server computer.
- **Pages only load images from Tohyee** (and LINZ's aerial map tiles). If you've put an image from another website into Tohyee's pages, it won't show.
- **Not yet reviewed by an accountant:** the income tax module, livestock reporting, expected sick leave and the farm calendar are new. Check figures before relying on them.

## New

- **Income tax (first stage, not yet reviewed by an accountant):** Accountant › Income tax works out taxable income from the books for a company or sole trader with a 31 March balance date: mark accounts as not deductible, entertainment (50%), business use % or not taxable, and add other adjustments, losses, other income and tax credits. It forecasts this year's tax from actuals plus the overall budget, shows standard provisional tax instalments and the expected end-of-year tax, and lists due dates (moved to the next business day). Type IRD's assessed RIT, filing date and payments in. It doesn't file returns or pay IRD (decision 545).
- **Tax depreciation and Investment Boost (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** give each fixed asset its tax method and IRD rate on the Income tax page, and claim Investment Boost (20%) on new assets first available from 22 May 2025. Tohyee works out each year's tax depreciation, depreciation recovered or loss on sale, and the adjusted tax value, and uses them instead of book depreciation in taxable income. Low-value assets up to 1,000.00 can be written off (decision 546).
- **IR10 from the books (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** once an income year ends, the Income tax page fills in the IR10 financial statements summary (all 60 boxes) from the books, with checks that it agrees with the profit and loss and balance sheet, and a CSV download. Change which box an account goes in, and mark drawings, dividends and current accounts (decision 547).
- **Imputation credit account (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** companies can keep their ICA on the Income tax page. Income tax paid comes in from the payments already typed there; type dividends (credits capped at 28/72), dividends received, RWT and refunds. A debit balance at 31 March shows the further income tax and 10% penalty due 20 June (decision 548).
- **IRD documents and your AI:** upload IRD assessments and statements on the Income tax page. Your connected AI can read them and propose the figures (assessed RIT, filing date, credits, payments); you check each proposal against the document and accept or decline it. Nothing changes until you accept, and Tohyee never asks for your myIR password (decision 549).
- **IRD payments from the books:** choose the account you code income tax payments to, and payments to IRD from the bank show on the Income tax page with a suggested income year, to count with one click instead of typing them (decision 550).
- **Income tax for other balance dates:** farms and businesses with balance dates other than 31 March (like 31 May) now get their own income years, provisional tax dates and end-of-year tax dates (decisions 551, 557).
- **Use-of-money interest estimate:** the Income tax page shows the interest IRD would charge so far, and if everything's paid on its due date, at IRD's current and past rates (decision 552).
- **IR4 imputation return boxes:** the imputation credit account now shows the figures for boxes 41-45 of the company return's imputation section.
- **IR4 income and tax boxes (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** once a company's income year ends, the Income tax page shows the company return's boxes 20B-30L: taxable income, tax, credits, provisional tax paid, and the refund or tax to pay (decision 553).
- **IR3 income boxes and tax calculation (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** once a sole trader's income year ends, the Income tax page shows the individual return's boxes 24-32 and the IR3 guide's tax calculation worksheet, down to the refund or tax to pay (decision 554).
- **Imputation credits and overseas tax (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** type imputation credits on dividends received and overseas tax paid separately from RWT and PAYE on the Income tax page. They bring the tax to nil but aren't refunded, and fill their own boxes on the IR4 and IR3. If you'd already typed imputation credits into "Tax credits", move them across (decision 555).
- **Next year's provisional tax on the returns, and imputation credits brought forward (not yet reviewed by an accountant):** the IR4 and IR3 boxes now include next year's provisional tax on the standard option (this year's RIT plus 5%, when it's more than 5,000.00). Sole traders can type excess imputation credits brought forward from IRD's letter (decision 556).
- **Whole dollars:** taxable income on the IR4 and IR3, next year's provisional tax and the IR10 boxes now drop their cents, and income tax is worked out on whole dollars of taxable income (decision 557). IRD's forms and guides don't say whether to round or drop the cents, so this is marked as not confirmed (decision 565).
- **Pasture and feed (not yet reviewed):** Farming › Pasture and feed records cover readings (typed or imported from a CSV or Excel file), each paddock's growth, the farm's average cover and feed wedge on any date, grazings with what was eaten, and supplements fed. It doesn't judge covers or make feed plans (decision 559).
- **Your AI and farm data:** a connected AI can now read soil tests, fertiliser plans and pasture records, and work out what a proposed fertiliser application would come to (tonnes, nutrients, cost) without saving anything (decision 560).
- **Farm apps (not yet reviewed):** make a key under Farm › Farm apps for an app you build or use, choosing exactly what it may read or record. Apps talk to Tohyee's new Farming API, which can't reach the books. There's a guide, and a ready-made soil sampling app that records samples with your phone's location; they show on Soil tests (decision 561).
- **Expected sick leave in the leave liability (not yet reviewed):** under Payroll › Leave › Liability you can record how many days of carried-over sick leave someone is expected to take beyond their new entitlements, with the reason. They're valued at a usual day's pay and posted with the rest of the leave liability. Tohyee doesn't estimate them (decision 564).
- **Livestock held for sale as a current asset (not yet reviewed):** in Livestock settings, choose which classes are held for sale. The next valuation moves their value to a current asset account, so the balance sheet splits livestock into current and non-current. Nothing approved is re-posted (decision 566).
- **Livestock and the reporting basis (not yet reviewed):** choose special purpose or general purpose (NZ IFRS or PBE Standards) reporting in Livestock settings. Under general purpose, the herd scheme revaluation always goes to profit and loss, and the valuation reminds you that tax values need your accountant's fair value adjustment. The draft financial statements' livestock note now states where the revaluation goes (decision 567).
- **Farm calendar (not yet reviewed):** Farm › Farm calendar reads vet visits, deliveries, stock trucks, contractor work, deadlines and meetings out of your own Gmail or Outlook email and shows them by day, week and month, with anything unclear kept aside for you to check. Only you see your mailbox and calendar. It hasn't been tried with a real mailbox yet (decisions 570-572).

## Changed

- **Remote access pauses itself when someone is guessing passwords:** 20 failed sign-ins through remote access within 10 minutes pause signing in from outside for an hour (longer if it happens again the same day), and server admins are emailed. The local network keeps working, and customers' payment and form links aren't affected. Resume early under Remote access on the server computer (decision 542).
- **Two-step sign-in after a changed server key:** if the server's secret key changes so an authenticator key can't be read, the password alone no longer lets someone set up a new authenticator. Use a backup code, the emailed reset link, or ask a server admin to reset two-step on the server computer. Putting the right key back makes the old authenticator work again (decision 543).
- **Unused credit:** month-end close now warns when a supplier or customer has credit (a credit note or overpayment) that could go against something they owe, and the Pay bills and Receive payments screens point it out before paying (PC13).
- **Safer links from other websites:** opening an invoice's PDF or print page from a link on another website no longer makes its online payment link, and pages only send forms and load images where Tohyee expects (decision 568).
- **Clearer screens (from a usability review, #323):** the farm calendar shows an item's details beside the schedule (or in place of it on a phone, with Back), says when it couldn't load instead of "nothing to check", and its filter is now called Sender. Bank lines open with **Review**; **OK** stays the one button that reconciles. The cash flow forecast leads with the lowest balance, any shortfall and a balance chart, with the bank accounts and assumptions folded away. Date fields say when a date can't be read, and Alt+Down opens the calendar from the keyboard.

## Fixed

- **Provisional tax after a late return:** when last year's return was filed late and an instalment went unpaid, the year's provisional tax was overstated (the shortfall counted twice), so the expected end-of-year tax looked too low. Each instalment now shows any shortfall it catches up (decision 558).
- **Provisional tax without an extension of time:** when last year's return is filed late and there's no tax agent's extension of time, the instalments now stay at last year's tax plus 5%, as IRD's standard option page says, instead of switching to two years ago plus 10% (decision 562).
- **Consolidation wording:** the decisions said consolidation never changes profit. That's only true of the automatic eliminations; an elimination adjustment can take out profit on stock one group company sold another that's still on hand, and there's now a worked example (decision 563).
- **IR10 drawings after a year-end journal:** boxes 56 and 57 now still show the year's dividends and drawings when they're cleared into equity or a current account on the last day of the year (decision 569).

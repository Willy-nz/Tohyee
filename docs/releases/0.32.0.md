Everything merged since 0.31.0 (#637–#648).

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations 0266 and 0267.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the compilation report wording, the default notes and the layout editor's details are Claude's. Have an accountant read the compilation report and notes before you rely on them.
- **Revenue recognition has moved:** Shopify's "Sales when sent" choice is now Settings › Revenue recognition. Nothing about how it works has changed.

## New

- **Annual financial statements as one document:**
  - the pages are a cover, the contents (made automatically, with page numbers), a compilation report, the four statements, the notes, and the depreciation schedule last (#644);
  - the compilation report's default wording follows the parts of CA ANZ's SES-2 example report, in Tohyee's own words, with [square brackets] to fill in. It can be left out (#644);
  - the depreciation schedule comes from the fixed asset register, with opening value, additions, disposals, depreciation and closing value (#644);
  - on screen it shows one page at a time, with Previous and Next. **Edit** makes every word editable, including "Prepared with Tohyee". **Save as draft** marks every page DRAFT, and **Save as final** doesn't (#645);
  - click an amount to see the transactions that make it up, even when not editing (#643);
  - the default notes cover IRD's minimum requirements (the Tax Administration (Financial Statements) Order 2014): grossed-up interest and dividends, a reconciliation to taxable income, associated persons, and shareholders' current accounts (#646).
- **Layout editor:**
  - one toolbar (Text, Table, Section, Columns, Page break, Up, Down, Delete, Export, Cancel and Update layout);
  - the report is on the left, and **Details** for whatever you click is on the right, with a **Preview** tab beside it. Page breaks can go after a table or note (#647);
  - **Add column** adds Date, Budget, Formula, Notes, Percentage and Variance columns. Variance can show "% of second" (#648).
- **Remove & redo** on a bank account unreconciles lines and voids what they created, after a preview, so they can be coded again. Payments are voided only if you tick it. The AI can do it at Full access (#642).
- **Revenue recognition** in Settings: for each Shopify store, recognise sales when paid or when fulfilled (#641).
- **Reports menu:** General ledger and Annual financial statements are now in the menu (#638).
- **Delete drafts** from the Drafts and Archived report lists, one at a time or several at once (#639).

## Fixed

- **Offline pages:** an organisation that doesn't farm no longer gets farm pages when the internet drops (#637).
- **Bank reconciliation report:** items already in the bank's balance (dated before the statement lines start, or after the last line) are shown on their own and no longer counted as "not yet on the statement" (#640).

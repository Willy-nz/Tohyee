Everything merged since 0.32.0 (#650–#666), including the fixes from the 11 Nov issue list.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations 0268 to 0275.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the worked examples for these changes are Claude's, not yet checked by an accountant.
- **Gift card GST is counted when the card is sold**, which is Inland Revenue's general rule for face value vouchers. Spending a card takes that GST back off the order, so it isn't counted twice.
- **Shopify stores already connected** keep making stock items for products Shopify tracks. Untick "I keep stock in Shopify" under What comes in if you make things to order.

## New

- **Shopify gift cards** are held in a liability until they're spent. Choose the account under the store's Gift cards, Set up. Orders refused earlier for a gift card are tried again (#663, #664).
- **Voucher products as gift cards:** tick the products that are vouchers (ones whose names say gift card or voucher are offered). From the next order, their sales are held as gift cards too. Earlier voucher sales stay in sales (#666).
- **Afterpay and other gateway deposits:** the bank reconcile screen matches each deposit to the order it pays for in the gateway's clearing account, and posts the fee with no GST (IS 26/02). It never matches by amount alone (#663).
- **"Do you track stock in Shopify?"** is asked when connecting. Orders stuck for no stock show one warning, with **Make these products non-stock** (#663).
- **Recode in place:** Edit on spend or receive money changes its coding, and **Recode several at once** does up to 100 together, after a preview. The statement line stays matched (#657).
- **Void** for manual journals, one or several, after a preview (#655).
- **Sales when sent** for orders already invoiced: choose them on Settings › Revenue recognition, and what's not yet sent is held (#655).
- **Reporting names** for accounts, shown on financial reports and statements (#651).
- **The AI can void manual journals posted through any AI connection**, at Full access, after a preview, with an optional day limit. It can also discard draft journals an AI made, unless a person has edited them (#650, #666).

## Fixed

- **Voided and corrected pairs** are left out of the Journals list and ledger reports unless you ask for them (#656).
- **Bank reconciliation report:** pairs that cancel out in the same month are hidden from "not yet on the statement". The totals don't change (#663).
- **Analytics file locked:** Tohyee waits briefly, then says to restart instead of showing a server error. The Windows installer stops leftover Tohyee processes when it updates (#663).
- **Shopify customer names** no longer come in doubled (#655).
- **Analytics reference lines** with a value and no kind are drawn as constant lines (#655).

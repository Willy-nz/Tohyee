## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds core migration 0024 and tenant migrations 0227–0230, 0233, 0246–0248, 0251 and 0252.
- **AI apps:** reconnect Tohyee in your AI app after updating, so it sees the new tools for Analytics reports, signing and rainfall.
- **Email:** signing requests, access reminders and the Hire help invite are sent through Settings › Email. Set that up first, or nothing is sent and the screen tells you so.
- **Not yet tried for real:**
  - signing emails sent to a real mailbox;
  - the AI tools for Analytics reports, tried with a real AI app;
  - the farm screens used on a real phone in the paddock.

## New

- **Sign documents online (e-signatures):**
  - **Quotes:** customers sign a quote to accept it. You then make the invoice or sales order yourself; Tohyee doesn't make it straight away any more (#586).
  - **Year-end:** directors sign the year's financial statements (#587).
  - **Staff:** employment agreements and farm safety inductions. A signed induction counts as the farm sign-off (#588).
  - **Any PDF:** Sales › Signing, with 1–20 signers and reminders (#589).
  - **Security:** each signer gets a one-time link and a 6-digit email code. The signed PDF has a certificate page, and **Check a copy** tells you if a file has changed since it was signed.
  - **Not for:** wills, powers of attorney, affidavits, statutory declarations, cheques, or anything that must be witnessed. The note is checked against the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017 (#590).
- **AI builds Analytics reports:**
  - your connected AI can see your data sources, try queries, make and change reports, and refresh sources (refresh is for owners and admins);
  - every chart is checked before it's saved;
  - it can't delete reports, change sharing or connect sources (#592).
- **Report history:** every Analytics report has a **History** button, so you can put back any earlier version, whether a person or the AI made the change (#592).
- **Time-limited access:**
  - give someone access from one date until another, and it stops by itself, including their AI and app connections;
  - you and they get a reminder 3 days before it ends, with Extend and End now buttons;
  - a new page shows everything that person did (#596, #606).
- **Hire help:** Settings › People and access › Hire help writes a job brief from your books, with counts and periods only and no names or amounts. **Invite them** then fills in the access and dates (#597).
- **Farm mobs:**
  - Farm › More › Mobs, with move, split, merge, sell, buy, deaths, births, weigh and change class;
  - sales, deaths, births and purchases update the livestock head count for tax;
  - stocking rate (SU/ha and head/ha), mob weights with gain, CSV import of mobs, and a way to turn typed mob names into mob records (#595).
- **Rainfall:**
  - rain gauges on the map, and a quick daily reading from the Log that works offline;
  - a 9 am reading counts as the day before;
  - history by month and season, beside the same time last year, plus CSV import (#594, #605).
- **Farm team tasks:**
  - who it's for, the due day, repeats, and a paddock or map pin;
  - phone reminders, and tasks on Today, the calendar and the map;
  - only bookkeepers and above can cancel a task (#600, #604).
- **Map extras:** troughs, gates, sheds, tanks, fences and pipes, a distance and area measuring tool, and layer buttons (#602).

## Changed

- **Settings › Email:** guided account setup and a separate page for each template, instead of one long page (#585).
- **Analytics shows words, not codes:** account types such as "Current liability", account classes, and invoice and bill statuses (#598).
- **Farm links:** a farm link sometimes said the farm was archived until you reloaded the page. Fixed (#602).

## Fixed

- **Mob counts stay in step with the books:** a livestock movement made from a mob can no longer be voided on its own, which left the mob count wrong. Use the mob's new **Reverse** step instead, which corrects both together. It's refused if the mob has since moved, split or merged (#611).
- **Class changes:** a class change linked to a livestock movement must match its new class, or nothing changes (#611).
- **"What they did":** no entries are skipped between pages when many actions happen at the same moment (#610).
- **Task assignees:** people whose access has ended, or hasn't started yet, can't be given tasks. Their open tasks show **Needs reassigning** (#610).

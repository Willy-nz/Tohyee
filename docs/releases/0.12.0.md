## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migrations 0176–0185, and core migration 0016).
- **Farm staff see only the farms chosen for them.** In an organisation with two or more farms (archived ones count), viewers, bookkeepers and report viewers now see and record only for the farms chosen for them on People and roles, or for All farms. **Someone not assigned yet sees no farms**, so after updating an owner or admin should choose farms for each person; People and roles badges anyone waiting with "Sees no farms". Owners and admins still see every farm. An accountant on the bookkeeper role needs All farms. The farm notebook and Analytics' farm tables need All farms (decision 744).
- **Get your backup recovery kit.** Backups now use a recovery key of their own, made by the first backup after updating. Every page reminds a server admin until the kit (a recovery code, or a recovery file with a passphrase) has been saved and checked under Server settings › Backups. Without it, a backup can't be restored on a replacement computer. Older backups still open with the key they were made with (decisions 730–739).
- **Menus have moved.** The Accountant menu is now three menus: Accounting, Tax and Settings. Every page keeps its address. Income tax has its own pages: Overview, Forecast, Tax calculation, Payments, Returns and Tax settings (decisions 700–705).
- **The mountain photo is gone** from Home (decision 701, replacing 598).
- **"Forecast" on Analytics charts is now "Linear projection"**, and it's only drawn on a regular run of dates. Saved charts keep the setting, but a chart that isn't grouped by date in order, or uses Top N, no longer shows a projection and says why (decision 692).
- **Not yet reviewed by an accountant or by Jess:** IRD account file imports, the farm-by-farm benchmarks, the backup and restore design, and the Analytics calculations (quick calculations, year to date and last year, relationships between tables). Check figures before relying on them.
- **Not yet tried for real:** backups to a real OneDrive, the recovery kit and the restore wizard on a real replacement computer, and IRD imports with real myIR files.

## New

- **Analytics, much closer to Power BI and Looker Studio** (#376 phases 1–4, #387):
  - **Formatting:** six report themes checked for colour-blind readers and the organisation's own themes; defaults per chart type; copy format; formatting for each chart and key figure; conditional formatting and table styles (decisions 668–675).
  - **More charts:** waterfall, funnel, gauge, bullet, scatter and bubble, histogram, box plot, treemap, heat map, calendar heat map, Sankey, small multiples, a matrix with nested columns, and New Zealand filled maps and maps with points; text with live values, images, shapes and buttons (decisions 676–687).
  - **Pages and interactions:** report pages with tabs; click a chart to filter or highlight the others; drill down by financial year, quarter, month and day; drill-through; slicers synced across pages; page filters; bookmarks; page and back buttons; a zoom slider on time charts (decisions 750–760).
  - **Calculations:** "Show as" running total, % of total, row or column, rank, change from previous and moving average; financial year, quarter and month to date from your balance date, the last 12 months, and the same dates last year; calculated fields shared by every report on a table; what-if sliders and switches; relationships between a table and its lookup tables, checked so amounts are never counted twice; sort by another column, default summaries and formats, and field folders (decisions 770–775).
  - **Prepare data:** pivot, unpivot other columns, fill down and up, replace values, remove duplicates, conditional column, date parts (including financial year), sort, index column and a formula column, each showing its effect on the row count (decisions 790–798).
  - **Trust:** what each value means, totals worked out from the underlying values (10/100 and 90/300 total 25%, not 20%), currencies kept apart, as-at balances never added across dates, merges that would repeat rows refused, the data's age shown under the report's dates, report versions with Undo and Redo and a warning when someone else saved first, and the access contract written down and tested (decisions 690–698).
- **Several farms in one organisation:** one farm selector shared by maps, soil, pasture and fertiliser, boundary imports that name their farm, see all farms compared on area, pasture freshness and fertiliser, benchmark by farm through a tracking category, and keep staff to their own farms (decisions 740–744).
- **Backups you can prove:** each backup's fingerprint recorded and checked weekly, honest status (off-site copies always "not verified"), a recovery kit, and a restore wizard for a replacement computer; scheduled jobs stay paused on a restored copy until a server admin turns them on (decisions 730–739).
- **Income tax: import IRD account files** (CSV or Excel) through a mapping. Each row waits for a person to accept it before it counts, and payments already in Tohyee aren't counted twice (decisions 710–716, as delegated, not yet reviewed).
- **My mail: file an attachment** into a bill, invoice, contact, asset, soil test or co-op shareholding, with a preview first. Rules only suggest; the owner confirms each one (decisions 720–726).

## Changed

- **Farm calendar:** mailbox setup is behind Manage mailboxes, and with two or more farms, an email that names no farm still goes under the mailbox's usual farm but waits in Needs checking, so it can't land on the wrong property unseen (decision 741).

- **The website** has a new look.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migrations 0210 and 0211).
- **Google Search Console needs one extra permission:** the Google app used for Google Analytics also needs the Search Console API turned on in its Google Cloud project. No new sign-in address is needed.
- **Meta needs your own free Meta developer app** (Analytics › data sources › Meta has the steps). Development mode is fine for your own Page and Instagram.
- **Not yet tried for real:** Meta with a real Page, Instagram or ad account, Google Search Console with a real website, and the new pictures on a real phone. They were checked in a browser at phone, tablet and desktop sizes, in light, dark and high contrast.

## New

- **Pictures to find your way:** the app switcher (Accounting, Farm, CRM, Analytics), the Farm menu and the phone's Farm tabs (Today, Map, Safety) have drawn icons. Sales and Purchases list their pages as bigger rows you can tap, with a picture and a line saying what each is for. Screen readers skip the pictures and read the labels, and who sees what hasn't changed (#510).

- **Google Search Console in Analytics:** connect your Google account, pick your verified website, and Tohyee loads up to 16 months of daily searches, pages, countries and devices (clicks, times shown, click rate and average position). The newest few days are marked "may still change". A ready-made **Website search** report shows clicks over time, top searches and pages, and searches that are shown a lot but rarely clicked. A help page explains adding your site to Search Console, including on Shopify (#512).

- **Meta in Analytics (Instagram, Facebook Page and ads):** a setup page walks you through making a free Meta app, and **Check setup** says plainly what to fix. **Connect Meta** asks only for read access; pick your Page (its Instagram comes with it) and optionally an ad account. Tohyee loads daily Page and Instagram figures, how each post did, and ads by campaign in the account's currency, plus a ready-made **Social media** report. Meta's limits: Instagram account figures go back 90 days and follower history starts at the first load; Instagram reports "views", not impressions; the Page goes back 2 years and ads about 13 months. The sign-in lasts about 60 days; Tohyee warns you 14 days before and reconnecting is one tap (#516).

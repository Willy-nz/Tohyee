## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migrations 0163–0170, 0172, 0174 and 0175).
- **Farm calendar mailboxes stop reading until you choose folders.** Each mailbox now reads only the Gmail labels or Outlook folders you tick. Mailboxes connected before this release stop being read, with a notice on the calendar, until folders are chosen under Mailbox settings (decisions 584-586).
- **Provisional tax payments need allocating.** A payment now counts towards an instalment only once it's allocated, confirmed by an IRD document or with evidence you type. Payments typed before this release show as "awaiting reconciliation" until you allocate them on the Income tax page (decisions 620-623).
- **New Google sign-ins for Google Analytics:** an admin adds `/api/analytics/google/callback` to the Google app's sign-in addresses, turns on the Google Analytics Data API and Admin API in its Google Cloud project, and adds the `analytics.readonly` permission. While the app is in Google's "Testing" stage, Google ends each sign-in after 7 days.
- **Not yet reviewed by an accountant:** provisional tax allocation, the co-op share register and the built-in Analytics values (Sales to Days to get paid) are new. Check figures before relying on them.
- **Not yet tried for real:** the farm calendar folder choice and My mail with a real mailbox; Google Analytics with a real website; MySQL and SQL Server with a real database; the Shopify changes with a real store.

## New

- **My mail (owners only):** turn on "Keep a private copy of these emails for me and my AI" on a farm calendar mailbox, using the same ticked folders. Only that owner can search and read the copy (admins, other owners, accountants and server admins get "not found"); it's stored encrypted, and `docs/PRIVATE-MAIL.md` says plainly what that does and doesn't protect against. The owner can let their own "Look only" AI keys read it, with each answer citing the email. Disconnect keeps the copy; "Delete imported history" removes it (decisions 610-619).
- **Farm Home:** with Farming on, Home is a farm dashboard: this week's calendar and Needs checking (private to you), stock by class, pasture cover and growth, fertiliser planned against applied, working expenses per kg MS, hectare, cow and stock unit, a small money card and the farm notebook. Cards with no data are hidden; "Business overview" opens the old Home (decisions 587-589).
- **Start dashboards by kind of person:** owners, sales people and farms each start with cards that suit them; your own Customise always wins (#360).
- **Farm calendar: your own items, repeats, sharing and a calendar link:** add items by hand with private notes; repeat every 1, 2 or 4 weeks or monthly and skip a date; share chosen items with the farm team after a preview of exactly what they'll see (never the email or your notes); and subscribe from your phone, Google, Apple or Outlook calendar with a private link (decisions 573-575).
- **Co-op share register (not yet reviewed):** Farm › Co-op shares records holdings by co-op and share class, every movement with its source statement, opening and closing figures for any year, and dividends, rebates and retentions separately. It's in the year-end pack. Nothing posts to the ledger, and there's no Fonterra minimum-share calculator yet (decisions 630-633).
- **Analytics data sources:** Microsoft SQL Server, PostgreSQL and MySQL databases (read only; PostgreSQL tested against a real database), and Google Analytics website data (daily visitors, sessions, pages, traffic sources, countries and devices) with a Website report template (decisions 602-609).
- **Analytics charts:** a Format section (colour sets, colours per value, red below zero, data labels, legend, axes, number format, stacked and 100% stacked, donut total, card title and border) and a second axis; key figures that compare with the previous period, last year or a target with an arrow and sparkline; more widths, Small/Medium/Large heights, duplicate, text and heading cards, click to edit and drag to reorder; and calculated values such as `[Sales] - [Costs]`, saved values, built-in book values (Sales, Gross profit, Net profit, Accounts receivable, Days to get paid and more, as Xero's reports define them, not yet reviewed) and report templates (decisions 590-597).
- **Analytics pickers and filters:** plain names grouped by source, date presets (this month to the last financial year, from the balance date), Yes/No instead of true/false, and filters for is one of, is blank, between, the last N days or months, this or last financial year, and "or" (decisions 576-578).
- **CRM:** move records from one rep to another with a preview first; leads' emails kept and replies turning into a follow-up task; account plans with goals and each person's part; renewal dates with reminders; and the CRM's tables in Analytics (decisions 650-654).
- **One look for every graph:** Analytics, Home, the cash flow forecast and the farm pages share one set of colours and styles in light and dark, checked for colour-blind readers. Colours you'd already set on a chart are kept (decisions 599-601).

## Changed

- **Farm calendar reads only the folders you tick** (see Before you update). Unticking a folder, or moving an email out of the ticked folders, deletes what was taken from it (decisions 584-586).
- **Provisional tax (as delegated, not yet reviewed by an accountant):** an excess paid towards instalment 1 now reduces instalment 2 in full (in ITX6, 6,367.60 is still needed by instalment 2, not 7,967.60). Each payment keeps its bank date and IRD's effective date, and is allocated to an instalment as confirmed by IRD, with evidence, or as a forecast assumption that never counts as paid (decisions 620-623).
- **Menus:** long menu columns have sub-headings, and Settings has an All settings page of cards like Xero's (decision 580).
- **CRM top bar:** seven tabs instead of seventeen, with every page still there, and Shopify reachable from CRM Settings and CRM Home (decisions 581-582).
- **Shopify and WooCommerce:** Shopify uses API version 2026-10 and stops with a clear message if Shopify withholds customer details; WooCommerce refunds come off the right order line (decisions 665-666). Square was looked at and left out: it doesn't take card payments in New Zealand (decision 667).
- **Home header:** uses Jess's own Aoraki / Lake Pukaki photo (decision 598).
- **Handover notes:** the old "Open questions for Jess" list points to where each was answered (decisions 634-639).

## Fixed

- **Analytics:** negative money shows as -$27.00; a value's label follows its column until you type your own; two things that could make "+ Create report" need two clicks (decision 576).

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations 0262–0264.
- **AI apps:** no new tools. Your connected AI can read cash flow and changes in equity layouts in custom reports.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the details of the new report layouts (cash flows and changes in equity), the annual financial statements report and the new custom report starts are Claude's calls. Check them before you rely on them.

## New

- **Annual financial statements are a report:** Reports › Annual financial statements shows the four statements and the notes, this year and last year side by side.
  - The screen and the PDF are built the same way, so they match.
  - Each statement has **Edit layout** beside it and follows your default layouts.
  - Edit the notes in place (#625).
- **Edit layout for cash flows:** rename lines and headings, group lines, move an account between sections, reorder, hide and add notes.
  - Lines you don't place stay in their usual section, so the net change always matches the bank.
  - **Set as default layout** makes Reports › Cash flows and the annual statements use it (#626).
- **Edit layout for changes in equity:** rename, reorder, group, hide and add notes to rows, and rename the start and end lines.
  - Lines you don't place still show, so equity at the end always matches the balance sheet.
  - **Set as default layout** makes Reports › Changes in equity and the annual statements use it (#631).
- **General ledger report:** Reports › General ledger shows every account with its opening balance, lines, running balance and closing balance (#629).
- **Custom reports can start from every report:**
  - trial balance, stock valuation, bank reconciliation, budget vs actual, changes in equity, cash flows, general ledger and the annual financial statements;
  - **Save as custom report** is on each of those reports too (#630).
- **Journals list:**
  - shows manual journals only by default (other kinds are under **Kind**);
  - 100 a page;
  - click one to open and edit it. Saving posts a correction, as before (#627).

## Fixed

- **Opening a bank transaction:** clicking a bank transaction in Account transactions or the journal report now opens that transaction, highlighted, with **Recode** open, instead of the reconcile screen (#628).
- **Custom list reports:** a nil opening balance shows 0.00 instead of a blank, dates show as dates, and amount columns line up on the right (#631).
- **Server stability:** a database connection ended while in use no longer crashes Tohyee, and closing a connection pool no longer waits forever (#623).
- **Offline farm records:** records saved within the same millisecond keep their order (#623).
- **Build server:** the tests that kept timing out now finish, so changes get through faster (#623).

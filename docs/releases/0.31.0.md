## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migration 0265.
- **Sales when sent is off until you turn it on:** Settings › Sales platforms › your Shopify store › Sales when sent. It applies to orders brought in from then on. Orders already invoiced stay in sales.
- **Not yet tried against a real Shopify store.** Watch the first few orders after turning it on.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the details of Sales when sent are Claude's calls. Check them before you rely on them.

## New

- **Shopify sales when sent** (Shopify only; WooCommerce isn't covered yet):
  - a paid order is still invoiced when it's paid, so GST is counted then, as before;
  - with Sales when sent on, the lines still to be sent, and the shipping, are held in Income in advance;
  - when Shopify marks a line sent, it moves to sales and its stock comes off, dated the day it went. Shipping goes with the first thing sent;
  - a refund before sending comes out of Income in advance, with no stock moved;
  - lines Shopify says don't need shipping are sales straight away (#633).
- **Mark as sent:** for an order Shopify never marked fulfilled, a bookkeeper can mark it sent in Tohyee on the day it went. It counts as sales, and its stock comes off, just as a fulfilment would. The sync log says who did it (#634).
- **Sales › To send:** a live list of Shopify orders not yet fully sent, with the customer, date, total and whether it's not sent or part sent. With Sales when sent on, it also shows what's held and has Mark as sent (#635).
- **Sales menu:** Documents is now in two sections, Invoicing and Orders (#635).

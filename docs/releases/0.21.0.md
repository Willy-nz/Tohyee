## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts** (tenant migration 0210).
- **Google Search Console needs one extra permission:** the Google app used for Google Analytics also needs the Search Console API turned on in its Google Cloud project. No new sign-in address is needed.
- **Not yet tried for real:** Google Search Console with a real website, and the new pictures on a real phone. They were checked in a browser at phone, tablet and desktop sizes, in light, dark and high contrast.

## New

- **Pictures to find your way:** the app switcher (Accounting, Farm, CRM, Analytics), the Farm menu and the phone's Farm tabs (Today, Map, Safety) have drawn icons. Sales and Purchases list their pages as bigger rows you can tap, with a picture and a line saying what each is for. Screen readers skip the pictures and read the labels, and who sees what hasn't changed (#510).

- **Google Search Console in Analytics:** connect your Google account, pick your verified website, and Tohyee loads up to 16 months of daily searches, pages, countries and devices (clicks, times shown, click rate and average position). The newest few days are marked "may still change". A ready-made **Website search** report shows clicks over time, top searches and pages, and searches that are shown a lot but rarely clicked. A help page explains adding your site to Search Console, including on Shopify (#512).

## Changed

- **Microsoft sign-in library** updated to version 7 (#506). Tohyee doesn't use it directly; Outlook mail and calendar sign-in are unchanged.
- **A test that failed at random** now checks the right thing (#511).

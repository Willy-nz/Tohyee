# Farm dairy effluent records

Issue #487 (Jess, 29 October 2026): "Do we have an effluent module… so we can
upload timestamped photos as well with reminders and whatever and so we
follow the rules. Select irrigators and types and maintenance etc." Farms
under **Otago Regional Council** (ORC) and **Environment Southland** (ES).
Decisions 1070-1099.

**Tohyee keeps the farm's effluent records. It doesn't decide what the law
or a consent needs, it never says a farm complies, and it never sends
anything to a council.** The farm enters its own consent conditions. The
starting templates only fill in what each council's published pages say,
name the page, and are marked "check against your own consent". Everything
drawn from those pages is **not yet reviewed by Jess**.

## Where it lives, and why (decision 1070)

The farm workspace has five tabs (Today · Map · Log · Safety · More), and a
phone's bottom bar has room for five. Effluent is a daily job for some farms
and none for others (runoffs, drystock), so it doesn't get a sixth tab:

- **More › Effluent** (`/operations/farm/effluent`): its own overview and
  screens, each with its own address, the same way Safety works. Setup (the
  systems, the map and the consent) lives here.
- **Today** (later parts of #487) shows what's due from Effluent (shift the pods, read the pond,
  checks, servicing, the consent's expiry and the WoF), and **Log** has
  quick buttons for an irrigation, a pond reading and "not applied today",
  so the everyday entries are one tap from where the farmer already is.
- The **farm calendar** (later parts of #487) shows Effluent's due dates beside Safety's.
- An effluent system's checks, faults and "out of use" are Safety's
  equipment checks, so a machine that's out of use shows in Safety too.

## Setup (decisions 1071-1079)

Owners and admins keep the setup (More › Effluent › Systems, map and
consent). Everyone who sees the farm reads it.

- **Systems** have a type: travelling irrigator; pods or K-line; slurry
  tanker or spreader; fixed sprinklers or rain gun. Each is a machine in
  Safety's equipment register (and through it, optionally, a fixed asset).
  Adding a system with no machine adds one, with a starting pre-start
  checklist for its type from DairyNZ's staff guides (critical items put it
  out of use). A system names the pond it draws from, the blocks it applies
  to, its settings for working out depth (entered from the farm's bucket
  tests or designer), how often it's checked (each day it's used, or each
  week), when to remind to shift it, its servicing interval, and the next
  Effluent WoF.
- **Ponds and sumps**: what they hold (m³), the gauge reading when full,
  the marks on the gauge, warning levels, and about how much goes in a day.
- **Effluent blocks**: paddocks, or an outline drawn on the map, with an
  area (or the paddocks' effective area) and lower- or higher-risk soils.
- **No-apply zones and buffers**: waterways, drains, wetlands, bores,
  boundaries, houses, roads and public places, as a point or an outline,
  with the buffer from the consent. On the map a point's buffer shows as a
  light red circle (a picture to plan by, not a survey).
- **Consent conditions**: number, expiry, most depth and rate, days between
  applications, nitrogen, storage, buffers, when not to apply, records the
  consent needs, who to tell and when, and anything else as written.
  Starting from the ORC or ES template fills in buffers, when not to apply,
  records, who to tell and other conditions from that council's pages,
  lists those fields as "still as the template had them", and links the
  pages. Depth, rate and return period are never filled in by a template.

Every setup record is a version in the safety history: dated by the
database, never changed or deleted, a correction says what was wrong, and a
record entered by mistake is withdrawn (it stays, marked).

## The daily log (decisions 1080-1089)

Farm staff, and bookkeepers and above, record on their farms: More ›
Effluent › **Irrigation log** and **Ponds and storage**, or from the Log
tab's Effluent buttons (shown on farms with a system). Everything is saved
on the device first and sent through the phone's one queue, so it works
with no signal and is kept once however often it's sent.

- **A run**: the system, block, paddock and run or line; started (and, if
  it's already finished, stopped); where the pods or irrigator were set
  (the phone's place, if allowed); weather and soil notes; photos (each
  keeps the camera's time, the phone's time and, if ticked, where it was).
  **Shifted** adds a position with its time; **Stopped** needs the
  **ponding and runoff check** (none, ponding or runoff, and what was seen
  if any). A tanker or spreader run records loads and the area.
- **Depth as entered and as worked out.** The person may enter a measured
  depth. Tohyee also works one out from the system's settings when it's
  saved, and keeps it with the settings used (a later change to the
  settings never rewrites an old run): travelling irrigator, flow ÷ (width
  × speed); pods and sprinklers, rate × the longest time at a position ×
  the share of time running; tanker, loads × tank ÷ area (10 m³ on a
  hectare is 1 mm). Both show side by side, labelled.
- **Last moved**: each pod line's or irrigator's last position and time
  shows on the log's map and on Farm › Map, with "shift due" from the
  system's shift interval.
- **Pond readings** against the pond's gauge, with a photo: about how full,
  storage left (worked out as if the sides were straight, so a guide) and
  days of storage at the daily inflow entered; **High** and **Low** at the
  pond's warning levels; **Not read today**.
- **Not applied today** (deferred irrigation): too wet, raining, frozen or
  snow, pond too low, system out of use, or other, with a note.
- **A system out of use** in Safety can't be recorded running until it's
  released (Jess, 29 October 2026).
- **Corrections**: the person who entered a record, or a bookkeeper or
  above, can correct it (with why) or withdraw it; nothing is deleted.

### Compared with the consent, in neutral words

The log counts, for the last 30 days: runs over the most depth entered (as
entered, else as worked out); runs by a system whose rate is over the most
rate entered; runs on a paddock sooner than the days between applications
entered; ponding and runoff recorded; positions inside a buffer on the map
(a point's distance from a bore or house, or from a drawn outline); runs
recorded while out of use; runs stopped with no ponding check; runs with no
depth. A limit not entered is "not compared". Each run lists what to look
at. **These count the records against the conditions as entered. They
don't say whether the farm complies.**

## Checks, faults, servicing and incidents (decisions 1090-1098)

More › Effluent › **Checks, faults and servicing** and **Incidents**.

- **Checks** are Safety's pre-start checks on each system's machine ("Do
  the check" opens it in Safety). Systems are checked each day they're used,
  or each week, as the setup says.
- **Report a fault** between checks: what's wrong, a photo, and whether to
  stop using it. "Don't use it" keeps it as a Safety check with that fault
  as a critical item, so the system is **out of use** until an owner, an
  admin or someone named on the machine releases it in Safety, saying what
  was done (never through an AI or app). Either way an action goes to
  whoever looks after it. A fault reported isn't counted as a check.
  Tohyee's "out of use" is a record, not a lock.
- **Servicing, repairs and the Effluent WoF** are records of work done: when,
  who did it, what was done, the report or invoice as a photo or PDF, and
  the next one (as the assessor said, or the system's interval from the last
  service). DairyNZ's Effluent WoF is voluntary and not recognised by
  councils; Tohyee only keeps its dates.
- **Incidents**: spill or overflow, ponding, runoff, inside a buffer, over
  the depth or rate, power failure, pump or pipe failure, or other; when,
  which system, what happened, what was done straight away, whether it
  reached water, photos, and **actions with the person responsible** for
  each and a date. Actions are Safety's, so they're on that person's Today
  and are marked done and checked in Safety. An incident closes (a
  bookkeeper or above, in person) once its actions are checked and closed.
- **Telling the council**: the incident shows the consent conditions'
  contact and when to tell them, and "Council not recorded as told" until a
  person records that they did (when, how, who they spoke to, reference).
  **Tohyee never contacts a council.**
- **Reminders**, worked out each time (never stored): shift the pods or
  irrigator (from the last move and the shift interval), read each pond (not
  read today), the weekly check, the pre-start check of a system used
  today, servicing within 14 days, the Effluent WoF within 30 days and the
  consent's expiry within 90 days. They show on **Today** (only on farms with
  effluent), on Effluent's overview, and the dated ones (servicing, WoF,
  consent expiry) on the **farm calendar** under Effluent due. Incident
  actions are under Safety due. No phone notifications yet: Tohyee has no
  notifications to send them with.

## The council pack, the weekly copy and tanker runs (decision 1099)

- **Council pack** (More › Effluent › Council pack; owners, admins,
  bookkeepers and viewers on their farms, not Farm staff): a zip for a date
  range, made the same way as the WorkSafe pack. `pack.pdf` starts with a
  **summary**: the consent as entered (and whether some conditions are still
  as the template had them), the counts against the entered limits in
  neutral words, pond readings (range and high readings), days not applied
  and why, incidents (and whether the council was told), applications by
  paddock, and how many depths were entered or only worked out. Then every
  effluent record with each version (who, when as set by the database),
  plus the systems' machines and checklists, their Safety checks, faults and
  releases, and the incidents' actions. `applied-areas.svg` shades the
  paddocks by applications, with no-apply zones and recorded positions.
  `files.json` and `files/` hold the photos with the camera's time, the
  phone's time and the phone's place. `chain.json` and `verify.mjs` check it
  as for the WorkSafe pack. **Tohyee doesn't send it anywhere.**
- **Weekly copy**: Safety › Records and settings › Weekly copy has
  **Include the effluent records** (off unless chosen). From the next copy
  on, the email and PDF are "Farm safety and effluent records". Turned on
  later, it doesn't go back for effluent entries already past the last copy.
- **Tanker or spreader runs through tractor mode**: tractor mode has a new
  operation, **Effluent (tanker or spreader)** (tenant migration 0201).
  Recording a tanker run, choose the tractor job: its paddocks, times and
  worked area (a phone GPS estimate) fill the run; loads × tank ÷ area gives
  the depth worked out.

## Records that say what's known (#491)

Following #491 (William's review of how records read after an incident):

- **Every effluent record is versioned.** Each change is a new version with
  its full details (so the old and new values are both there), who made it
  and the time Tohyee saved it; a correction says what was wrong; a record
  entered by mistake is withdrawn, kept and marked. Nothing is overwritten.
- **Reported, checked, done, verified.** An incident is the reporter's
  account. **Checked it** (a bookkeeper or above, in person) records what was
  found, beside the report, not instead of it. Its actions are marked done
  and then checked and closed (Safety's workflow). A reported fault with
  "don't use it" takes the system out of use straight away; only a release
  in Safety puts it back.
- **Entered, estimated, measured.** Depth as entered is what the person
  measured or said; the depth from the system's settings is labelled an
  estimate. Positions and tractor areas are phone GPS estimates. A position
  near a waterway or inside a buffer is shown as that, never as a finding
  that effluent reached it.
- **Saved isn't told.** Work on the device shows Saved on this device or
  Waiting to send until Tohyee has it; an action is assigned in Tohyee, and
  Tohyee doesn't message anyone or say anyone has read it. "Council told" is
  only what a person records after telling them.
- **Exports say their scope.** The council pack's summary starts with its
  scope (farm, dates, what's included and not), how many versions are
  corrections and how many records were withdrawn. The hashes show records
  weren't changed after they were saved; they don't show the contents are
  true, and the records aren't privileged because of them.

## Worked checks (tests)

`tests/integration/farm-effluent-setup.test.ts`:

- **EF1** a system is a machine in Safety's register with a starting
  checklist for its type; settings its type doesn't use aren't kept.
- **EF2** sending a new system twice makes one system and one machine.
- **EF3** owners and admins keep the setup; bookkeepers and farm staff
  can't; farm staff read it on their assigned farm only.
- **EF4** a council template fills in its pages' conditions, marked, and
  leaves depth, rate and return period to the consent; changing a field
  takes it off "from the template".
- **EF5** changes are versions; a correction needs why; a stale change is
  refused; the database refuses updates and deletes; the history checks out;
  withdrawn records are hidden unless asked for.
- **EF6** a failed critical check puts the system out of use until someone
  allowed releases it (Safety's rules, unchanged).
- **EF7** effluent records stay out of the WorkSafe pack and the weekly
  copy, but are in the same history.
- **EF8** the setup route answers farm staff on their farm.

`tests/integration/farm-effluent-log.test.ts`:

- **EF9** a travelling irrigator run keeps depth as entered and as worked
  out; changing the settings later doesn't rewrite it.
- **EF10** stopping needs the ponding check; ponding needs what was seen;
  times can't be backwards or in the future; a run needs a place.
- **EF11** pods: shifts are positions with times; depth from the longest
  position; last moved and shift due; no shift after the stop.
- **EF12** a tanker run's depth is loads × tank ÷ area.
- **EF13** the comparison counts over depth, too soon, in a buffer, ponding
  and recorded while out of use (the pods failed a critical check).
- **EF14** pond readings give percent full, storage and days left, and the
  high warning; days not applied need a reason.
- **EF15** farm staff record on their farm only; viewers can't; only the
  person or a bookkeeper or above corrects; withdrawals keep the history
  whole.
- **EF16** the phone's queue sends a photo, a run, its shift and its stop
  once each, though three answers were lost; the shift and stop find the
  run's id from the first answer.

`tests/integration/farm-effluent-care.test.ts`:

- **EF17** a fault puts the system out of use with an action for whoever
  looks after it; sending twice makes one; a minor one doesn't; release
  only by someone allowed, never an AI key.
- **EF18** servicing and the WoF: who or what is needed; the next ones from
  the last; reminders soon.
- **EF19** reminders: shift, pond, weekly check, pre-start check of a system
  used today, in order; a weekly system isn't "not checked today" in Safety.
- **EF20** an incident with actions and their people; the council prompt
  comes from the consent; "council told" is a person's, never an app's;
  nothing is emailed.
- **EF20a** checking an incident is a bookkeeper's, in person, kept beside
  the reporter's account (#491).
- **EF21** closing needs its actions checked and closed, by a bookkeeper or
  above in person; the history checks out.
- **EF22** farm staff report on their farm only; corrections are the
  reporter's or a bookkeeper's.
- **EF23** the calendar's dates; incident actions come through Safety's.

`tests/integration/farm-effluent-pack.test.ts`:

- **EF24** the pack holds the effluent records, the systems' machines,
  checklists and checks and the incident actions, not other safety records;
  the chain checks out; photos keep their phone time and place.
- **EF25** the summary counts against the entered limits in neutral words;
  the map shades the applied paddock; the zip has every part.
- **EF26** Farm staff can't download it; the route serves an owner a zip.
- **EF27** the weekly copy leaves effluent out unless chosen, keeps the choice
  when saved again, and says "safety and effluent" when it's in.
- **EF28** a tanker run from a tractor job takes its paddocks, times and
  area; only tanker runs; an unknown job is refused.

`tests/unit/effluent-pack-map.test.ts`: shades, escaping, empty map.

`tests/unit/effluent-reminders.test.ts`: months, service and WoF dates,
reminder order, calendar dates.

`tests/unit/effluent-log-rules.test.ts`: depth for each type, times,
pond state, buffers, and neutral summary wording.

`tests/unit/effluent-setup-rules.test.ts`: templates name their sources
and leave the limits to the consent, every type has settings and a
checklist, the consent in force, buffer circles, and screen addresses.

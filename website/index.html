Features

Everything a New Zealand business runs on.

From the first quote to the GST return and the pay run, in one place, with the same care for the numbers your accountant has.

Bank feeds and reconciliation

Akahu for NZ banks, SimpleFIN for overseas banks, Stripe, PayPal and Wise, or statement files from a folder or mailbox. Bank rules with conditions and splits fill in the everyday lines.

GST, the NZ way

Invoice, payments or hybrid basis. The GST101A return set out box by box, with an audit report listing the documents behind every figure.

  • Monthly, two-monthly or six-monthly
  • Month-end close locks the period

Sales

  • Quotes, invoices, credit notes
  • Pay now links (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Emailed from your own mailbox
  • Shopify and WooCommerce orders

Purchases

  • Bills, purchase orders, expense claims
  • A bills inbox from your mailbox
  • Duplicate bill warnings
  • Approval rules for who signs off

Payroll

  • PAYE, ACC, KiwiSaver, student loan
  • Holidays Act leave and timesheets
  • Payslips, bank files, payday filing

Reports that explain themselves

Profit and loss, balance sheet, aged receivables and payables, budgets, custom layouts, a cash flow forecast and consolidation across organisations, with commentary a person checks.

Analytics and a built-in CRM

Bring in CSV and Excel files alongside the books, shape them into dashboards and pivots, and share them with clients. A CRM with Gmail and Microsoft 365 email and calendar sync is included.

Stock and assets

  • Weighted average cost by location
  • Fixed asset register, depreciation

Foreign currency

  • Invoices, bills and bank accounts
  • Realised and unrealised gains

Safe by design

  • Two-step sign-in for everyone
  • Posted history can't be edited
  • Each organisation in its own database

Bank reconciliation

Statement lines arrive from your bank feed and Tohyee suggests the invoice, bill or rule that fits. Confident matches are one click, and anything unusual is easy to code with splits and transfers.

  • CSV, Excel, OFX, QIF, CAMT.053 and MT940 files too

Install

Up and running in minutes.

Put Tohyee on the computer that will keep your books. Everyone else signs in from their browser.

Windows

  1. Download TohyeeSetup and run it.
  2. Keep the setup token from its last page.
  3. Open Tohyee from the Start menu, create your login and organisation.
  4. Save a copy of your backup key somewhere safe.

Docker

One image for each release, with a ready compose file (PostgreSQL included) and scripts in the release's Docker zip.

# the image for each release
docker pull ghcr.io/willy-nz/tohyee:0.7.0

Linux server

A server bundle to run with Node.js 20.19 or later. Point it at PostgreSQL 15 or later, set two environment variables and start it; put it behind HTTPS.

# after unpacking the bundle
export DATABASE_URL=postgres://…
export TOHYEE_SECRET_KEY=…
PORT=3000 ./start.sh

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is it really free?

Yes. Tohyee is open source under the GNU AGPL v3. There's no subscription and no limit on organisations or people. Some connections use other services that may charge, such as your bank feed provider or Stripe's card fees.

Where is my data kept?

On the computer or server where you install Tohyee, in a PostgreSQL database for each organisation. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you connect a service, such as a bank feed or your email account.

Can my accountant or team use it?

Yes. Add people with a role (owner, admin, bookkeeper or viewer). Everyone signs in from their own browser with two-step sign-in, from the office or, with remote access, from anywhere.

Does it file my GST return with IRD?

Tohyee prepares the GST101A return and the audit report behind it. You file the return yourself in myIR. Payroll makes the payday filing file to upload.

What happens if my computer dies?

Tohyee makes encrypted backups every night into a folder you choose, such as one synced to OneDrive. Restore them on a new computer with your backup key, which is why you should keep a copy of the key somewhere safe.

Can I move from another accounting system?

Yes. Import contacts, products, a chart of accounts and opening balances from CSV, and bring in bank statements from files or a bank feed.

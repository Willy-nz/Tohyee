From the paddock to the year-end

Draw or import your paddocks, plan the fertiliser, count the stock and value it for tax. Your own AI can read the herd app's export and turn it into a dashboard, without touching the books.

Paddocks and fertiliser

Import paddock shapes or draw them, with LINZ aerial photos behind. Plan nitrogen by paddock, link the bills, and see planned against applied in kg N/ha and dollars.

Livestock for tax

Movements and head counts that reconcile, herd scheme and national standard cost values, agent account sales, and the year-end journal with its workings.

Your AI, on a short lead

Leave it the herd app's spreadsheet and it builds its own tables and dashboards. Movements it finds are suggestions until you accept them.

On the job and at the counter

Tohyee works in a phone's browser, so there's nothing to install for your team. A visit shows the site, the notes and the checklist. The shop tablet clocks people in with a PIN, and their hours land on their timesheet for approval.

The tablet never signs in to your books. It holds its own key, and that key can only clock people in and out.

Year-end, in one go

Special purpose financial statements with last year beside them and your own notes, as one PDF. The accountant pack puts the trial balance, ledger and bank reconciliations in a single ZIP.

  • Statements of financial performance, position, changes in equity and cash flows
  • Draft notes to start from, marked as drafts until you save them
  • Recode a posted line with a reason instead of voiding the invoice

Accounting you can check

New accounting apps are racing to do the books for you. Tohyee helps too, and it shows its working, so a person stays in charge of what goes into the books.

Suggestions wait for you

Bank matches, bank rules, bills read from your inbox and drafts from an AI assistant are suggestions. They reach the books when someone says OK.

The database says no

PostgreSQL itself refuses a journal that doesn't balance and any edit to posted history. Mistakes are voided or reversed, so the trail stays complete.

Written down before it's built

Every accounting rule starts as a worked example with real numbers. More than 2,000 automated tests run them against a real database before any change is accepted.

AI on a short lead

AI is optional and uses a key you make. Keys can only look until you allow more, and no AI tool can delete, void or reopen anything.

A place of their own for your records

Tohyee runs on your own computer or server, like a media server at home. Your team and your accountant open it in their browsers, each with their own login and role.

  • Each organisation has its own PostgreSQL database, so it can be backed up, restored or moved on its own.
  • Backups run every night, encrypted with a key you keep somewhere else.
  • Remote access through your own Cloudflare domain or Tailscale, with two-step sign-in.

Get Tohyee

Start with a test organisation, and keep a copy of your backup key away from the server.

Windows

  1. Download and run TohyeeSetup.
  2. Use the setup token it shows to make your first login.
  3. Create an organisation in the server settings.
  4. Save your backup key somewhere else.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Tohyee is open source under the GNU AGPL v3, with no subscription or per-user fee. Your hosting, bank feed, payment provider or other connected services may charge for their own part.

Where does my data live?

On the computer or server you choose, with a separate database for each organisation. If you connect a bank feed, payment provider, remote access or AI assistant, that provider sees what it needs to do its job.

Can my team and accountant use it from elsewhere?

Yes. Set up remote access with your own Cloudflare domain or Tailscale Funnel, then give each person their own login and role. Server settings only open on the server itself.

Does it file with Inland Revenue?

Not directly. Tohyee works out the GST return with an audit report and makes payroll payday filing files. You check them and submit them to IRD yourself.

Does livestock cover every NZ tax situation?

No. Pigs, associated-person transfers, long-term bailments and a few other cases aren't supported yet, and Tohyee refuses them rather than guessing. Check the scope with your accountant before relying on a valuation.

What if I need to restore my books?

Restore an organisation from a nightly backup as a new copy, then check it before switching over. Keep backups off the server, and keep the encryption key separately: without it the backups can't be opened.

Tohyee is early and built in the open. Try a test organisation first, check reports and returns before relying on them, and keep your own backups. It comes without warranty under its licence and isn't endorsed by Inland Revenue. Report security concerns through the security policy.

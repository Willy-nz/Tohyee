Accounting that runs on your own computer.

Tohyee is free, open-source accounting software made in New Zealand. It covers GST returns, invoices, bills and bank reconciliation. Your books stay on your computer, and there's no monthly fee.

Free under the GNU AGPL v3 licence. Also available for Docker and Linux. Still early: please read before you start.

What it does

Sales

  • Quotes, invoices and credit notes
  • Repeating invoices
  • Customer payments and statements
  • Pay now links through Stripe or PayPal
  • Shopify and WooCommerce orders
  • Print or save as PDF

Purchases

  • Bills and supplier credit notes
  • Purchase orders
  • Paying several bills at once
  • Expense claims and mileage
  • A bills inbox and approval rules

Bank

  • Bank and credit card accounts
  • Statement import, from a folder or mailbox too
  • Bank rules and bank reconciliation
  • Bank feeds: Akahu for NZ banks, SimpleFIN, Stripe, PayPal and Wise

GST and reports

  • GST return with an audit report behind every box
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance
  • Aged receivables and payables
  • Custom reports and budgets
  • Cash flow forecast and consolidation

Stock and assets

  • Products and services
  • Stock at weighted average cost, by location
  • Fixed asset register and depreciation

Bigger organisations

  • Departments, classes and locations
  • Custom fields and projects with time tracking
  • A built-in CRM with email and calendar sync

Payroll

  • Pay runs with PAYE, KiwiSaver and student loan
  • Holidays Act leave and timesheets
  • Payslips, bank files and payday filing

Analytics

  • CSV and Excel sources, the books and CRM
  • Dashboards and pivots
  • Shared with clients or emailed as reports

Install on Windows

  1. Download TohyeeSetup from the latest release and run it on the computer that will keep your books.
  2. The installer's last page shows a setup token. Keep it open for the next step.
  3. Choose Open Tohyee from the Start menu, use the setup token to create your first login, and set up your organisation.
  4. Tohyee starts by itself whenever that computer starts, and its icon sits by the clock. Save a copy of your backup key somewhere safe.

Docker and Linux instructions are in the read-me.

Before you start

  • Tohyee is free software provided as is, with no warranty, as set out in its licence.
  • It's still early. Try it on a test organisation before you put real books in it.
  • You're responsible for your own records. Check your GST returns and reports before you file or rely on them, and ask your accountant if you're unsure.
  • Keep your backups, and a copy of the backup key, somewhere other than the computer running Tohyee.
  • Tohyee isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, Inland Revenue or any other accounting software company.
